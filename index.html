WHAT IT CANNOT ESTABLISH

These selected cases cannot tell you how often an event will occur, how much money you will save, or whether a real control is effective. A missed legitimate deadline is an opportunity-cost signal in this model—not a valuation.

Real testing would need representative scenarios, reliable evidence and the actual workflow. A new scenario can change the conclusion.

A TRANSPARENT REHEARSAL

A small model.
An important distinction.

AI helped create the fictional scenarios and explanations in advance. This page runs deterministic rules over those prepared cases. No live model is connected, and it cannot generate or analyse a new scenario you type in.

Each case contains visible request facts, specified verification outcomes and a fictional underlying truth. Control decisions use the evidence, not that truth. The truth is used afterward to label whether a release was legitimate or a diversion.

Six cases are available initially. Two further prepared challenges expose limits of a design. This is an interactive thought experiment, not an independent evaluation or a forecast of real-world performance.

Source code is public. No live business systems, payments or customer records are connected. Your choices stay in this page; the brief is created on your device.
